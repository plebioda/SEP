Operations Inventory settings now have ranges, and a value outside one is refused: with 422 by the settings API, and at startup when it comes from `settings.yaml` or the environment. `SCHEDULE` runs from 10 minutes to 1 day in minutes, hours or days; `TASK_TIMEOUT` from 60 to 300 seconds; `POLL_INTERVAL` from 1 to 30; `REPO_TIMEOUT` from 2 to 30; `CONNECT_TIMEOUT` from 1 to 30; `MAX_CONCURRENT_PROBES` from 1 to 8; `RUN_RETENTION` from 10 to 1000; and `STALE_RUN_AFTER` up to a day. `REPO_TIMEOUT` and `CONNECT_TIMEOUT` together may take at most half of `TASK_TIMEOUT`. An override saved before this release that falls outside its range is ignored, and the deployment's value applies.
