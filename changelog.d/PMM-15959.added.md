Operations for MongoDB gains the shared step contract its managed-cluster operations dispatch against: a step declares a typed privilege, whether it is idempotent, and what it targets (a member, a replica set, a node, or the run); secrets reach a step only by reference through a fail-closed delivery interface that keeps the bytes out of the dispatch body, logs and result; and one executor adapter boundary submits by token, observes and cancels idempotently. The shared operation run and its dispatch receipts record each step dispatch before it runs, so a retried or failed-over dispatch re-sends the same executor token under the same key while a stale leader epoch is refused with nothing written.
